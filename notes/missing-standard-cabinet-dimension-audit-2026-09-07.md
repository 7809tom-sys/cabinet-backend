# Missing Standard Cabinet Dimension Audit

The active catalog was reviewed for Base, Wall, Tall, and Vanity records with a missing width, height, or depth after the owner-authorized standard-depth correction.

| Finding | Result | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Active records with a missing dimension | 21 | Reviewed without automatic completion. |
| Records eligible for an unambiguous standard-rule completion | 0 | No record changed. |
| Records retained in conflict status | 21 | Require product-specific source confirmation. |

The missing-value records are specialty or incomplete constructions, including blind-corner trays, shelf boards, tall panels, glass stackers, wall specialty components, and vanity records whose codes express width and depth rather than a verifiable height. No source-backed standard value can be inferred for those records from the owner’s general rule alone.
