# Pelican-Informed Product Organization Research

## Scope Guardrail

This research concerns **Pelican surface and fixture products only**. CabinetDealz cabinetry families, cabinet SKUs, prices, labels, and Designer cabinet navigation are expressly out of scope.

## Public Pelican Navigation Pattern

Pelican’s public navigation separates products into broad departments, then routes shoppers through product-type collections and practical subfilters. Its relevant public departments are **Kitchen**, **Bathroom**, and **Surfaces**. The Surfaces submenu exposes flooring, quartz, mosaics, surrounds, and surface accessories. Kitchen exposes sinks, faucet installation/type pathways, and kitchen accessories. Bathroom further distinguishes sinks, faucets, toilets, accessories, bathtubs, shower pans, surrounds, and glass doors.

| Pelican department | Example public collection path | Transferable CabinetDealz organization lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Surfaces | Flooring, Quartz, Mosaic Tiles | Start with a material department, then let shoppers choose a product type before narrowing specifications. |
| Kitchen | Retrofit Sinks, installation type, Kitchen Faucets | Use task-led entries such as *Kitchen sink* and *Kitchen faucet*, then show install and configuration filters. |
| Bathroom | Sinks, Faucets, Toilets, Bathtubs, Shower Pans | Distinguish fixture families first, then expose mount, compliance, drain, and size attributes. |

## Collection and Filter Findings

Pelican’s Flooring collection shows **48 products** and organizes refinement around product type, availability, series/collection, installation type, piece size, color tone, wear layer, thickness, and baseboard height. Quartz uses product type, availability, series/collection, installation type, slab size, and design type. Mosaic Tiles uses product type, availability, series/collection, installation type, material, application area, and waterproofing. Bathroom Sinks uses product type, availability, series/collection, installation type, material, mount type, and porcelain color.

The collection cards pair a readable product name with a concise physical descriptor. For example, the flooring list calls out plank size and installation type; quartz cards call out slab dimensions and thickness; mosaic cards call out sheet dimensions, materials, and intended wall/floor application; and sink cards call out overall dimensions with a clear type such as *Vessel Sink* or *ADA Compliant*.
