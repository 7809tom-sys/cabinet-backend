# Catalog quick-view validation

Validated against the live development preview on 2026-09-21.

After selecting the Aero Blanc cabinet line, the Base catalog rendered 48 cabinet cards and 48 **Quick view** controls. Opening `2-DB24` presented an accessible modal without navigating away from `/catalog`: the modal showed the catalog drawing, `W 24" · H 34.5" · D 24"`, two-drawer configuration, category/type/placement facts, four style-scoped accessory options, a fit-confirmation note, and a full-detail link retaining the selected style (`/catalog/2-DB24?style=390016`). The modal remained within the viewport with its own scroll region.

The same validation also found three orderable Builder Grade lines (FW, FG, FE) had been marked inactive despite 125–132 orderable source rows each. They were restored to active status; no price, cost, supplier mapping, or SKU availability was changed.
