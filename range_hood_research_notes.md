# Manufacturer Range-Hood Profile References

This research supports configurable range-hood profiles in Cabinet Config and the 2D designer. The following manufacturer sources establish representative profile categories and dimensions.

| Profile | Representative manufacturer model | Key dimensions | Designer use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Wall-mount chimney hood | ZLINE 587-30 | 30 in W × 21 in D × 13.9 in H; telescoping chimney | Wall-mounted canopy with a centered chimney above a range |
| Wall-mount chimney hood | THOR HRH3007 | 30 in W × 19.75 in D; adjustable 33.5–55 in duct cover | Taller adjustable chimney-style wall hood |
| Cabinet insert / power pack | Broan-NuTone BBN1303SS | 29.875 in W × 11.4375 in D × 2.8125 in H | Flush under-cabinet/liner insert profile |

The profile selector should not hard-code a specific manufacturer product. It should let an administrator select the mounting type, width, depth, body height, chimney height, finish/image, duct style, and whether the hood is an insert. These fields can reproduce a manufacturer-specific configuration when the product and image are entered in Cabinet Config.
