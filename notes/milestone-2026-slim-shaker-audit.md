# Milestone Slim Shaker Spec Book 2026 — catalog audit

**Source:** `/home/ubuntu/upload/884b4cfcfb.pdf` (42 pages). The document applies to five Slim Shaker style codes: **SWO** (Slim White Oak), **SDW** (Slim Dove White), **SIB** (Slim Iron Black), **SAO** (Slim Amber Oak), and **SAG** (Slim Aston Green).

## Source facts confirmed in the opening and wall-cabinet pages

The order syntax is `STYLECODE-SKU`; the source example `SWO-W0930` confirms that the door-style code remains a supplier/style overlay while the wall SKU determines physical geometry. The Slim White Oak and Slim Dove White construction page specifies full-overlay five-piece solid-wood doors and drawer fronts with MDF center panels, 3/4-inch solid-wood face frames, 1/2-inch cabinet-grade plywood boxes with finished exteriors, clear UV-coated natural interiors, dovetail solid-wood drawer boxes, concealed undermount full-extension soft-close drawer slides, and six-way adjustable soft-close hinges. The separate Universal Box page sets the alternate box-construction language for the remaining Slim lines; it does not alter master dimensions.

Wall diagrams verify 12-inch cabinet depth. Single glass door walls W1212GD through W2118GD are 12/15/18 W by 12/15/18 H. Double glass door walls W2412GD through W3618GD are 24/27/30/33/36 W by 12/15/18 H. Slim SWO and SDW use clear glass with color-matched interiors; SAG uses frosted glass with natural interiors. WER2412GD/WER2415GD/WER2418GD are 24 W × 12 D Easy Reach glass corner products and have **no shelf**. DCW2412GD/DCW2415GD/DCW2418GD are 24 W × 12 D diagonal glass corners and also have **no shelf**. These are exact SKU-family configuration rules; glass inclusion remains controlled by the distributor’s policy rather than source-wide prose.

## Verified source facts from visual pages 13–22

The standard 30- and 36-inch-high wall cabinets have two adjustable shelves and the 42-inch-high walls have three. Standard single-door walls are 9/12/15/18/21 W; double-door walls are 24/27/30/33/36/39 W. The source includes W3930, which must be treated as a 39-W cabinet if a supplier source line is added. Refrigerator uppers W30/W33/W36 use 24-inch depth; their 12/15/18-inch-high variants have no adjustable shelf and their 24-inch-high variants have one adjustable shelf. Wall drawer units WD1848/WD1854 have two adjustable shelves, while WD1860 has three.

The diagrams correct two important corner-wall configurations. DCW2430/DCW2436/DCW2442 diagonal wall corners are **24 W × 30/36/42 H** with two adjustable shelves for 30/36 and three for 42. WER2430/WER2436/WER2442 Easy Reach wall corners use the same 24-W and two/three-shelf rule. Their 24-in. corner-leg span is distinct from a standard wall cabinet’s 12-in. depth and must be represented by the corner-footprint logic rather than inflating a standard depth field. WBC2730/WBC2736/WBC2742 blind wall corners are **27 W × 30/36/42 H × 12 D**; the source model code is WBC27, not WBC24, and can be pulled for exact fit.

For base cabinets, standard B12–B21 have one drawer and one adjustable shelf. B24/B27 have one drawer and one adjustable shelf, while B30/B33/B36/B42 have two drawers and two adjustable shelves. Full-height base single-door models BT09/BFH12/BFH15/BFH18/BFH21 have one adjustable shelf; full-height two-door BFH24/BFH27/BFH30/BFH33/BFH36 have one. Two-drawer bases 2DB24/2DB30/2DB36 have two fronts. Three-drawer bases 3DB12–3DB36 follow the documented three-front layout. The source confirms blind-corner physical widths of **33/36/39 in.** for BBC36/BBC39/BBC42, respectively; 36/39/42 are installation ranges, not physical cabinet widths. This supersedes the earlier generic width interpretation for the current Milestone source.
