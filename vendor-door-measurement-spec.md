# Vendor Sample Door Measurement Specification

**Source:** user-provided [CabinetDealz Sample Door Measuring Sheet](/home/ubuntu/upload/CabinetDealz_Sample_Door_Measuring_Sheet.pdf), received 2026-09-27.

## Vendor entry fields

One measurement submission starts from one physical sample door and is entered in inches to the nearest 1/16 inch. The vendor may explicitly check every Door Style in its own supply line that uses the **same physical profile**; the source sample remains identified on the submission.

- Vendor, vendor style name, vendor style code, color/finish, measured-by person, date.
- Door style: shaker, slim shaker, double shaker, raised panel, beaded, slab, or other.
- Door sample width × height, door thickness, top/bottom rail, left/right stile, center rail.
- Panel: flat, raised, beadboard; outside edge profile and inside frame profile.
- Drawer front: 5-piece or slab; rail/stile width when 5-piece; slab-below height; thickness.
- Cabinet construction: face frame or frameless; overlay: full, partial, or inset; door gap and face-frame width.
- Photo evidence checklist: straight-on door, straight-on drawer front, edge profile close-up, back of door.

## Source and verification policy

- A vendor-submitted record is an **official vendor source** and records the vendor, vendor style code, submitter, and submission date automatically.
- Citation template: `Measured from sample door: [Vendor] [style code], measured by [name] on [date].`
- **Not Designer-live by vendor submission alone.** The vendor record remains `pending` internal confirmation.
- A CabinetDealz team member must measure the physical sample door once, check that confirmation, and enter team-measured rail and stile values before approval.
- After approval, every **vendor-selected same-profile Door Style** receives the team-measured Designer frame profile and the generated vendor-sample citation. No data is copied to unselected styles or unrelated supply lines.
