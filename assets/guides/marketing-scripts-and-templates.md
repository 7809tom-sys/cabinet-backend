## Text Message Scripts

### First Contact — Warm Lead (Someone You Know)
> "Hey [Name]! I just launched my own cabinet store — [Your Store Name]. We carry beautiful kitchen cabinets at contractor pricing. If you or anyone you know is thinking about updating their kitchen, I'd love to help. Here's my store: [your storefront URL]"

### First Contact — Cold Outreach (Contractor / Builder)
> "Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] with [Your Store Name]. I supply quality kitchen cabinets to contractors and builders at competitive pricing. I'd love to be your go-to cabinet source. Can I send you my catalog? [your storefront URL]"

### Follow-Up After No Response (Day 3)
> "Hey [Name], just wanted to make sure you got my message! Here's my store link again: [your storefront URL]. We have a 2D design tool so you can plan your whole kitchen before you order. No pressure — just wanted you to have it."

### Sending the Design Tool
> "I wanted to share something cool — there's a free kitchen design tool on my site where you can lay out your whole kitchen and see different door styles. Takes about 10 minutes and gives you a real price. Want to try it? [your storefront URL]"

### After Sending a Quote
> "Hey [Name], I sent over the quote for your kitchen. Let me know if you have any questions or want to tweak anything. I'm happy to jump on a call and walk through it with you."

### Referral Request (After Delivery)
> "Hey [Name]! Hope the kitchen is looking amazing! If you know anyone else thinking about updating their kitchen, I'd love an introduction. Just forward them my store link: [your storefront URL] — I'll take great care of them!"
