# Skyline Door-Frame Source Log

## Official sources checked

| Source | Verified finding | Measurement status |
|---|---|---|
| Skyline Cabinetry product collections page | The official catalog groups collections as Shaker, Slim Shaker, Double Shaker, Classic, and Frameless European, with named finish/style pages. | No numerical stile or rail dimensions published on the collection page. |
| Skyline Cabinetry samples page | The official samples page identifies Cabinet Styles & Collections. | No numerical stile or rail dimensions published in accessible page text. |
| Skyline Cabinetry downloads page | The official downloads route is available. | Its accessible text did not expose a technical door-profile measurement document. |
| Skyline product guide PDF | The guide confirms cabinet naming and certain cabinet dimensions. | The extracted accessible text did not provide numerical door stile or rail dimensions. |

No Skyline door-frame dimension has been added or altered from these sources. Only exact official measurements will be matched to internal styles; visual classification alone is not treated as numerical evidence.

## Representative collection-page check

Official pages for Shaker White, Slim Dove White, Double Dove White, and Charleston White were each inspected. They require dealer authentication before exposing product information and did not disclose numerical stile, rail, or center-rail dimensions to public access. The public collections page confirms the named style families, but not the measurements needed to set them in the Designer.
