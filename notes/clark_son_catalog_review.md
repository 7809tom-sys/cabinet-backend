## Clark And Son 2025 catalog review

### Pages 1-4 findings
- Page 1 is the catalog cover.
- Page 2 is the 5-year limited warranty page.
- Page 3 contains the official Clark And Son color key and feature matrix.
- The color key on page 3 lists these official door codes only: ASP (Aspen White), WPG (West Point Grey), FS (Fresh Sage), RH (Rustic Hickory), NG (Winchester Grey), WS (White Shaker), CS (Carmel Shaker), HS (Hickory Shaker), PS (Platinum Shaker), and MBS (Midnight Black Shaker).
- Greek Villa does not appear in the page 3 color key.
- The page 3 matrix also supports WPG as a raised-panel door and the other listed painted/wood finishes as shaker-style lines.
- Page 4 text says Aspen White, West Point Grey, Winchester Grey, White Shaker, Platinum Shaker, Hickory Shaker, Midnight Black Shaker, Fresh Sage, and Carmel Shaker are full-overlay cabinet lines.

### Immediate implication
- Based on the first catalog pages alone, Greek Villa is not part of the main Clark And Son 2025 color key and may be a newer/unlisted finish, a different program, or a name used outside the catalog.

### Pages 5-9 findings
- Page 5 is the table of contents. It shows an internal section called "Cabinet Styles" on pages 1-3, then wall cabinets start on page 4.
- Pages 6-8 visually confirm the full Clark And Son cabinet style set shown in the catalog: Aspen White (ASP), Fresh Sage (FS), Carmel Shaker (CS), Hickory Shaker (HS), Winchester Grey (NG), Midnight Black Shaker (MBS), West Point Grey (WPG), White Shaker (WS), Platinum Shaker (PS), and Rustic Hickory (RH).
- No Greek Villa style appears anywhere on the Cabinet Styles pages.
- Aspen White and West Point Grey are the only clearly raised-panel styles shown on the cabinet style pages; the rest are shaker variants.
- Page 9 begins the wall-cabinet product section and is not a new finish/style page.

### Current conclusion after pages 1-9
The official Clark And Son 2025 catalog appears to contain exactly 10 named finish/style entries, and Greek Villa is not one of them. At this point Greek Villa looks like an off-catalog finish, a later addition, or a separate program rather than a listed 2025 catalog style.

### Sample doors page (catalog page 41) findings
- The official Sample Doors page shows individual door faces for exactly ten styles: ASP, HS, WPG, NG, PS, WS, RH, MBS, FS, CS.
- The Sample Doors page color key repeats the same ten style codes and names as the front color key.
- Greek Villa is NOT present on the Sample Doors page.
- Door-face confirmations from the sample page: ASP is a raised-panel white; WPG is a raised-panel gray; HS is a light natural hickory shaker; RH is a dark warm-brown rustic hickory shaker; NG is a warm greige/taupe shaker; PS is a medium cool gray shaker; WS is a bright white shaker; MBS is a deep black shaker; FS is a muted gray-green sage shaker; CS is a warm caramel/gold shaker.

### FINAL catalog conclusion
The Clark And Son 2025 catalog defines exactly 10 door styles (ASP, HS, WPG, NG, PS, WS, RH, MBS, FS, CS). Greek Villa is not in this catalog at all. Therefore Greek Villa should be treated as an off-catalog / newer finish and must NOT be added as a Clark And Son 2025 catalog style until the user confirms its code, our-name, and paint reference and whether it is truly a Clark And Son SKU.
