# Owner-Authorized Standard Cabinet Measurement Correction

The owner confirmed the following standard-family dimensions: Base and Tall cabinets use a 24-inch depth, standard Wall cabinets use a 12-inch depth, and standard Vanities use a 21-inch depth. Where a four-digit width-height segment is unambiguous, the first two digits are width and the next two are height.

## Applied correction

Ten active, plain vanity records were corrected from 24-inch depth to the owner-authorized 21-inch depth. The corrected set consists of one vanity base, two vanity drawer bases, and seven vanity sink bases. Their width, height, cabinet identity, availability, commercial data, and public-source privacy were not changed.

## Excluded records

No Base, Wall, or Tall record met both conditions of being a plain standard construction and violating the authorized rule. Records that are specialty, appliance, corner, refrigerator, panel, filler, table-top, desk, or multi-dimension constructions were intentionally left unchanged. In particular, refrigerator-wall and specialty codes that carry a 24-inch depth remain outside the standard Wall rule.

## Verification

After the update, every active plain Vanity Base, Vanity Drawer Base, and Vanity Sink record with a vanity placement is 21 inches deep. The original audit also confirmed zero stored width-height conflicts among 57 active records whose code patterns explicitly encode both measurements.
