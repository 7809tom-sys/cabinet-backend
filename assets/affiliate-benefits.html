How to Use the Kitchen Design Tool

The 2D Kitchen Designer is the heart of the platform. It lets you (or your customer) lay out a real kitchen to scale, apply a door style, and see live trade-based pricing as you build. Here is the workflow end to end.

1Set up the room. Type exact dimensions in feet and inches, choose a room preset, or drag on the canvas to draw the room outline. The plan redraws to scale with foot rulers so everything stays proportional.
2Pick the active wall. Highlight a wall (Top / Bottom / Left / Right). New cabinets and openings march neatly along the wall you select, snapping flush and edge-to-edge so the run lines up automatically.
3Drop in cabinets & appliances. Place items from the palette — Base, Wall, and Tall cabinets, plus appliances (range, refrigerator, dishwasher, sink, hood, over-range microwave). Base cabinets sit on the floor and wall cabinets mount at the correct standard height. Corner cabinets render as an L-shape, and blind corners auto-include the required filler.
4Choose the door style. Select a door style and the placed cabinets re-skin instantly to that look and finish color — shaker, raised panel, slab, beaded, inset, and more — so the whole kitchen previews in the chosen style.
5Refine the details. Move, rotate, and resize items; tweak dimensions; drop islands to the floor; and add finishing touches like crown molding, fillers, and toe kicks. Add doors and windows as adjustable wall openings. A tape-measure tool shows the exact gap between any two objects.
6Switch views. Toggle between Bird's-Eye, Front Elevation, and Split view. The elevation view draws each cabinet's actual door-style front, so customers see what the finished wall will look like.
7See live pricing. A running bill of materials prices every cabinet, filler, molding, toe kick, and countertop line as you design — with your member pricing and markup applied — so the quote is always current.
8Get a countertop quote. Add a countertop with your chosen material plus sink and faucet selections to fold surfaces into the same quote.
9Save, share, and export. Save the design to the customer's record, export an itemized Build Sheet (CSV), or print a graph-paper worksheet. Need help? Use the AI design consultant for layout suggestions.

Get a Professional Designer (and share the screen live)

If a customer wants expert help, they click "Get a Professional Designer" right inside the design tool, pick an open time slot based on your designers' availability, and book it. The booking lands in the CRM as a new deal with the design attached. During the session, the designer can paste a video/phone link for voice and generate a read-only "share live" link — the customer opens it and watches the kitchen layout update in near real time as the designer works.
