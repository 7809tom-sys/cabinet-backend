# MasterTrac cut-to-fit shelf planning audit — 2026-10-04

The owner's approved March 1, 2022 MasterTrac workbook remains the SKU and **List-price** authority. The generated public catalog retains 454 List-priced products and no dealer Invoice field. The purchased fixed/adjustable shelf SKU, finish, depth and ¾-in thickness never change when a user specifies a narrower installation opening. A cut is a placement attribute, not a new manufacturer SKU or a reduced product price. Historical List is a reference, not checkout.

The manufacturer’s [MasterTrac Floor Mount installation manual](https://sauder-closets.com/pro-resources) (cover dated 03/21/22, lot 579554) says on printed p. 41 to measure the finished opening between uprights, saw an adjustable shelf to length and drill through its adjustable-shelf template at the cut edge. Printed p. 44 says a fixed shelf may be cut to length and its connector holes rebored with the appropriate jig. Those pages support a **planning cut length**, not a fabrication guide embedded in the Designer. The older 2012 WoodTrac prepackaged-system instructions are not evidence for these 2022 432xxx shelf connectors.

Implementation scope: cut straight fixed and adjustable shelf boards **within connected floor-tower bays only**. Users choose a catalog stock width and enter a narrower installed clear width; all shelves in that bay share the opening. The adjacent panel positions, saved shelf widths, plan/elevation faces and 3D board solids follow the installed width. Materials show the purchased stock SKU, its unchanged List price, installed width and per-board offcut. Reopening a saved tower reconstructs its installed span and purchased stock width. The preview never calculates saw kerf, edging, drilling coordinates, load rating, labor, hardware or a different finish. Drawer and shoe concept bays cannot be combined with cuts because their fit is not verified at nonstandard clear widths. A conservative 12-in minimum for the planning UI is a software scope limit, not a manufacturer support specification.

No Continuous Wall Builder code or checkout pricing rules should change. Preserve the existing full-suite failure baseline and use an isolated QA identity for placement, editing, undo, reload and 3D proof.

## Implemented Designer flow

In **Closet → Plan connected towers**, choose a bay and click **Cut stock shelf to fit**. The width selector becomes the purchased stock SKU width and a separate Installed clear width input sets the panel-to-panel opening. The SKU takeoff identifies fixed and adjustable shelf stock numbers, counts, per-shelf offcut and historical List each; the 2022 materials subtotal remains explicitly **not added to checkout**. The committed Designer materials list gives a separate cut note for each purchased SKU and installed width. Editing the tower can revise or remove the cut, and one undo restores the previous assembly. Saved placement records preserve source stock width and final dimension; 3D meshes and elevation faces use the installed width.

Local production-preview proof used a two-bay Glacier White tower: four shelves in bay 1 use **24-in stock** fixed SKU **432529** (2 × $16.00 List) and adjustable SKU **432535** (2 × $11.00 List), cut to **22.5 in installed** with **1.5-in offcut per shelf**. The tower spans 48.75 in and the total materials reference is $231.00, unchanged from the full-stock SKU takeoff. A 3D mesh assertion measured the shelf solid at 22.5 in, while the selected board retained SKU 432529 and the cut metadata. Browser interaction changed the installed span to 21.75 in, confirmed 2.25-in offcuts, undid back to 22.5 in, then reloaded the draft with its stock SKUs and cut dimensions intact. The isolated preview is not by itself a claim that the public website is updated.

## Validation outcome

TypeScript and the first production build passed; 43 tests passed across the focused tower, catalog, elevation, concept-3D and checkout suites. A rendered elevation regression confirms that a 22.5-in installed board occupies 90 pixels at the 4-px/in scale while its stock 432529 SKU remains visible. The complete Vitest run recorded 103 failed cases versus the 102-case pre-cut baseline; the sole additional case was an unrelated AI consultant network/timeout test (`server/design.consult.test.ts`, closet fallback) that **passed when rerun alone**. Do not call the complete suite green. The isolated browser verified the purchase SKUs, unchanged $231.00 historical List subtotal, 3D board, 22.5→21.75-in edit, single-step undo, and saved-draft reload without page errors.
