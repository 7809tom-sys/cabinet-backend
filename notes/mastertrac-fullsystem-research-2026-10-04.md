# MasterTrac full-system source findings — 2026-10-04

## Manufacturer sources

The [official Sauder Closets Pro-Resources index](https://sauder-closets.com/pro-resources) exposes [MasterTrac Floor Mount installation, lot 579554](https://img1.wsimg.com/blobby/go/6f5bec3d-b8c4-4544-93cc-8216566afeda/downloads/MasterTrac%20Installation%20Floor%20Mount%20579554%20202.pdf?ver=1778077076178), [Hang Mount installation, lot 573359](https://img1.wsimg.com/blobby/go/6f5bec3d-b8c4-4544-93cc-8216566afeda/downloads/MasterTrac%20Installation%20Hang%20Mount%20573359%202023.pdf?ver=1778077076178), and [Closet Design Rules](https://img1.wsimg.com/blobby/go/6f5bec3d-b8c4-4544-93cc-8216566afeda/downloads/ed13b9ff-e1d1-4374-9d92-b2824dad6cb3/Closet%20Design%20Rules.pdf?ver=1778077076149). The Floor Mount manual's **cover is dated 03/21/22**, though the PDF binary was created in May 2023. It lists many exact SKUs present in the owner's March 1, 2022 List-price catalog, so it provides much stronger period-compatible installation evidence than the separate 2026 spec book. It does **not** automatically reconcile different numeric dimensions in the workbook.

Floor manual PDF pp. 3–9 list panels, straight/adjustable/shoe/corner shelves, rods and cups, fences, doors, drawer boxes/fronts, cleats, toe kicks, and their matching 2022 SKUs. Pp. 36–37 show generic open-topped small/large drawer-box construction and front/side-slide assembly, but not a SKU-specific scaled exterior model or complete drawer clearance. The lower drawer rail begins at upright hole 2; large drawers repeat at holes 10/18/26 and small at every fourth hole (p. 37). P. 38 shows shoe shelves angled between uprights and a front-edge fence, with rear dowels six holes below the top fixed shelf and front dowels eight holes below it in a floor-mounted configuration. P. 39 shows rods spanning between rod cups; rods are cut to fit and no diameter or cup envelope is specified. The Hang Mount manual uses relative hole 9/11 for the shoe shelf (p. 33). These are configuration-specific illustrations, not universal drilling or automatic bay-compatibility specs.

### Unresolved primary-source discrepancies

The 2022 workbook-derived catalog lists shoe shelf depth **14.5 in**, while the period-compatible floor manual prints **14 3/8 in** on p. 5. Drawer-front catalog fields have nominal 18/24/30-in width and 5/10-in height labels; the manual p. 6 prints exterior elevations **18½/24⅝/30½ in wide** by **4⅞/9⅞ in high**. Some door catalog dimensions/names disagree with the floor/hang manuals: e.g. workbook nominal 53-in door vs manual 52-in family with a 52¾-in actual leaf; one 30×53 workbook row gives a 12¼-in field rather than the manual's 15¼-in leaf. Drawer SKU 432393 lacks a primary catalog height. Rod/cup and shoe-fence cross-sections, drawer slide travel/clearances and door swing/hinge locations remain undocumented. Do not silently resolve these conflicts, fabricate 3D thicknesses/mountings, or convert 2022 List reference to checkout pricing. The older [owner MasterTrac brochure](https://www.owingsbrothers.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/MasterTrac-Custom-Closets-Catalog.pdf) confirms furniture-grade ¾-in system construction, four-sided drawers, rods and tiltable shoe shelves but is marketing-level. Another [2012 WoodTrac instruction PDF](https://homeimprovementsupply.com/Images/Sauder/WoodTrac.pdf) is for earlier prepackaged 414xxx kits—not proof of 432xxx MasterTrac interfaces.

A detailed two-stage assessment against the official 2022-era installation drawings concluded: exact SKU/List and non-placeable conceptual controls are supportable for drawers, drawer fronts, doors, shoe shelves/fences and rods/cups; automatic fit, collision-safe 3D and physical connection assertions still need manufacturer-controlled technical drawings/compatibility rules. A 2026 manufacturer book may corroborate roles but must not replace the 2022 SKUs, dimensions, prices or finishes.

## Reach-in bedroom closet template evidence

[Closet America dimensional guide](https://www.closetamerica.com/closet-measurement-guidelines-preparing-for-a-closet-customization/) describes reach-in closets at least **24 in deep** and common four-panel bi-fold openings of **48–72 in wide**, with typical door height **80 in**. [Ruffino Custom Closets](https://ruffinocustomclosets.com/standard-closet-size/) gives a typical reach-in width range of **3–8 ft** and depth **24–30 in**. [US Door & More bifold sizing guide](https://www.doornmore.com/blog/complete-guide-to-bifold-door-sizes/) likewise lists four-panel bifold sets in 48/60/72-in nominal widths and notes finished/rough openings vary with manufacturer. These are planning defaults, not product dimensions or a construction order. The owner specifically proposed ~6½–7 ft wide × 30 in deep with bi-fold doors. Therefore suitable **editable planning templates** are 78×30 in with centered 60-in nominal opening and 84×30 in with centered 72-in nominal opening (both nominal 80-in high). Do not render these as MasterTrac cabinet doors or assert a particular rough-opening allowance; ask users to measure the finished space and actual door.

## Implemented independently: editable reach-in starter rooms

The regular Designer's room-layout chooser now offers three nominal planning rooms: **72×24 in / 60-in bi-fold opening**, **78×30 in / 60-in opening**, and **84×30 in / 72-in opening**. Each creates a centered architectural four-panel *room* door on the front wall, 80 in tall, with 96-in room ceiling. It has no catalog SKU, no product price and no claimed rough-opening or hinge hardware spec. The 24- and 30-in room depths can be edited without the kitchen's 36-in minimum overriding them. Existing work prevents application of a fresh template; the owner must save and start a blank room to switch presets.

An isolated local production-preview test of the 84×30-in template found a saved 72-in front opening offset 6 in from both sides, changed the depth from 30 to 28 in without removing the opening, placed a valid shared-upright MasterTrac tower on the rear wall, saw the dated March 2022 List subtotal clearly marked **not added to checkout**, and confirmed a second preset is disabled while the tower occupies the design. The four bi-fold leaves are a generic translucent architectural planning cue; hiding the room walls in 3D also removes them and reveals the dimensioned board-level tower. The 3D proof is **local preview**, not a claim about the public domain. Kitchen hinged doors and Continuous Wall Builder are not modified.

**Outstanding full-system work:** The period-compatible installation manual establishes assembly concepts for drawers, shoe fences/shelves, rods/cups and doors, but its missing geometry/compatibility details and conflicting printed dimensions mean those accessories remain searchable, List-priced references, **not automatically placeable or safe 3D models**. A manufacturer SKU-to-bay fit/slide/hinge/rod/fence drawing set, or explicit owner approval of *non-engineering conceptual visualizations*, is needed before broadening the tower option set. Do not call the MasterTrac program complete yet.
