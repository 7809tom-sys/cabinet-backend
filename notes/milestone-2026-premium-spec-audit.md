## Verified source facts from visual pages 11–15

- Standard wall-cabinet cabinet depth is **12 in.** and the cabinet height is encoded by the SKU suffix.
- Standard framed wall cabinet door components have stated 3/4-in. depth; cabinet width / door width are shown separately, so master product width must remain the **cabinet** width, never the door width.
- W1212GD, W1512GD, W1812GD, W2112GD are single glass wall cabinets of 12 H; W2412GD, W2712GD, W3012GD, W3312GD, W3612GD are double glass cabinets of 12 H. W3012/W3312/W3612 double-door 12 H entries are marked **No shelf**.

- 24-H double-door walls W3024/W3324/W3624 have one adjustable shelf.
- 30-H single walls W0930/W1230/W1530/W1830/W2130 have two adjustable shelves; W1530GD/W1830GD are single glass with the documented glass/interior split. 30-H double walls W2430/W2730/W3030/W3330/W3630 have two adjustable shelves; W2430GD/W3030GD are double glass.
