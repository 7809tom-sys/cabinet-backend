# Altaeuro overlay filler source notes — 2026-10-18

## Official sources

- [U.S. Cabinet Depot Altaeuro Frameless Collection](https://www.uscabinetdepot.com/frameless-altaeuro-collection) identifies Altaeuro as a frameless full-access line and includes Torino White Pine in the Torino series.
- [U.S. Cabinet Depot Altaeuro Design Tips PDF](https://www.uscabinetdepot.com/media/wysiwyg/PDF/sales_materials/altaeuro_Design_Tips_V1.2021.pdf) states that Altaeuro fillers use a grey-linen backer plus a separate matching door-material overlay.
- [Official current V8 Altaeuro spec book](https://prod-uscd-product-files.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/file/altaeuro_Single_Pages_V8_DIGITAL_ONLY.pdf), pages 54–55, identifies the OF overlay-filler model family.

## Verified Torino White Pine (`TWP`) sellable overlay-filler models
