The regular Designer's connected-tower dialog now starts with a straight-wall start/end corner positioning decision, then offers each bay **Open shelves**, **Drawers · illustrated**, or **Shoe organizer · illustrated**. Drawer options are 1–3 nominal 5-in small slabs or 1–2 nominal 10-in large slabs/Shaker fronts. Shoe options are 1–4 shelf/fence pairs in Chrome or Matt Nickel.
