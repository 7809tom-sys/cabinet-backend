# MasterTrac Closet workspace: installation-rule audit (October 4, 2026)

**Source of truth for customer-visible List price and SKUs:** owner's March 1, 2022 MasterTrac worksheet, column E; public-only generated catalog `client/src/lib/mastertrac-catalog.json`. Never expose workbook column D (dealer Invoice).

**Installation source:** Sauder/WoodTrac floor-mount manual, March 2022 revision, obtained via the manufacturer's [professional resources](https://sauder-closets.com/pro-resources). Local research copy: `/home/ubuntu/mastertrac-reference/floor-install.pdf`. Page 23 says a **wall cleat is required between all uprights**. Pages 41–42 allow cutting and reboring a cleat for a nonstandard bay using the manufacturer template. The 2022 sheet carries one-each finished cleats in 12/18/24/30/36/42 inch stock sizes; e.g. Glacier White 24-inch `432608` List $9, Slate Rift 24-inch `431613` List $11.25, Spiced Mahogany 24-inch `432838` List $11.25. Count one per actual connected bay. Do not pretend its exact drilling pattern is a modeled 3D component.

**Curved corner:** 2022 sheet's 29.5 × 29.5 × 0.75 inch shelf bounding size; curve is explicitly illustrative by owner approval. The sheet has matching one-each H-joint channels **White `433172`, Slate `433174`, Brown `433176`**, each $8.50 List. Previous code accidentally paired Slate Rift corner with White; now correct to `433174`. Only a fixed corner and level adjacent fixed shelf get that joint reference.

**Rod and rod cups:** floor-mount manual page 39: insert tabs of two rod cups into designated upright holes, leave one exposed hole above the cups, cut rod to length, place in cups. Approved 2022 one-each 8-foot rods: Chrome `433223` List $29, Matt Nickel `432932` List $54. Ten-cup retail packs: Chrome `433225` List $30.50/pack and Matt Nickel `432934` List $30.50/pack. A true bay takeoff would need **two cups per rod** and `ceil(2 * rods / 10)` packs, not price two loose cups or one pack per bay. Pole diameter, hole elevation, load and cup/rod cross-section are not in the price sheet. Do not claim an engineering-grade rod mesh.

**Fasteners:** manual page 21 calls for three 2-3/4-inch screws into studs for its first floor upright; use three toggle anchors if studs are not available. Subsequent placement and wall cleat anchoring vary with design and wall substrate. The public sheet has fastener packs, but the amount is not safely inferable just from board count. Label fasteners/anchors as **installer verification required**, never silently invent quantities or mark them included.

All 454 SKUs are indexed; structural boards are placeable, the corner branch picker has not reached the public bundle, and rod SKU cards are disabled reference-only. An isolated QA email was used. ## Branch implementation (under verification)

`test/mastertrac-closet-workspace` adds one finish-matched wall cleat per connected bay, including a stock-width cleat with an explicit cut/rebore note for field-trimmed shelves. The fixed curved corner uses its real finish-matched one-each H-joint, including Slate SKU 433174. Cleats and H-joints are separately priced at the approved March 2022 **List** values; none enters checkout.

A per-bay **Hanging poles** choice uses a real 8-foot stock rod for each chosen level (Chrome 433223 or Matt Nickel 432932) and counts **two cups per rod** per the March 2022 installation manual. Cup stock is in packs of ten (Chrome 433225, Matt Nickel 432934); the reference takeoff rounds up each finish's total cup demand across the design, rather than charging a pack for each bay. Heights and bay clearances are user-selected; 3D rod diameters and cup shapes are **illustrative**, not drilled-hole or load drawings. A rod in an existing tower cannot overlap its fixed shelves or share a bay with a cut-to-fit shelf until that interface is verified.

The separate Closet-only 3D/front-elevation views, finish-first left catalog, and right-side parts chooser are being tested. Dragging verified individual rectangular boards to the measured floor plan already uses its original pointer-drop handler; shelf elevation also has a new Closet-only vertical pointer drag. The room's measured geometry is not quantized to an unconditional six-inch grid. Wall substrate, anchors, screws, exact cup-bore and cut-edge installation require an on-site installer and are **not** automatically priced.
