# AltaEuro / U.S. Cabinet Depot Frameless Release — Source Evidence

Source: **U.S. Cabinet Depot Specification Manual V8.2 — altaeuro Studio Frameless Collection**, supplied as `/home/ubuntu/upload/altaeuro_Collection_Full_Spec_Book_V8.2(1).pdf`, dated October 2026 in PDF metadata.

## Verified release facts

- Product key, pages 4–5: lists the seven source price families. **Syncron Slim Cava Rosales 3 = SCL** (page 4 product key, page 5 visual swatch). SCL is therefore a sellable finish, not a hold.
- Pricing & availability: the V8.2 table matches the staged V10 price audit exactly for every **115 geometry-verified source SKU** and each of the seven price columns; no list-price updates were required.
- Base cabinets: V8.2 pages 20–33 identify 30-1/4-inch box height and 4-1/2-inch legs; overall installed height is 34-3/4 inches. Pages 21–22 show standard door/drawer bases, pages 27–28 sink bases, pages 32–33 easy-reach bases.
- Walls: pages 37–48 show the printed 13-1/2-inch standard wall depth (with 24-inch refrigerator-wall exceptions), and the wall front configurations.
- Tall cabinets: pages 50–57.
- Vanities: pages 64–70.
- Door samples: page 5 is the source for the SCL (Rosales 3) door swatch. All generic cabinet drawings attached to the AEF line are cropped from the cited V8.2 family pages.

## Release boundary

- Only AEF distributor SKU rows, AEF distributor cabinet overrides, the AEF door styles, and the AEF distributor line are to be changed.
- `cabinets` shared master geometry and `catalogImageUrl` are explicitly outside the release scope; framed SKUs remain unchanged.
- The 162 V8.2 source SKUs without safe verified geometry remain unavailable and unpublished.
