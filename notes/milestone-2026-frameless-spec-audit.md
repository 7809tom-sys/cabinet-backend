# Milestone Frameless Spec Book 2026 — catalog audit

**Source:** `/home/ubuntu/upload/4d1d8ef6d3.pdf` (42 pages), supplied September 21, 2026.

## Scope and source controls

This is a **Frameless Line** specification book, separate from the previously audited framed, Slim Shaker, Premium, and Builder Grade books. The source identifies six finish/door style codes: Matte Ivory (`MI`), Matte Black (`MB`), Midnight Glass (`MG`), Crystal Glass (`CG`), Oak Blonde (`OB`), Oak Shade (`OS`), High Glossy White (`HW`), and High Glossy Gray (`HG`). `CG` and `MG` are glass-door finishes restricted to wall cabinets.

The audit will use this book only to correct source-verified product facts, construction information, dimensions, documented shelf/door counts, source availability, and source status. It will not infer new prices, replace supplier SKU mappings, alter aliases, overwrite other distributor overlays, or modify customer/order data.

## Verified source facts from pages 3–8

The frameless construction is **camlock-and-dowel**, with a **3/4-inch plywood box and shelves** finished in white melamine, a **3/4-inch plywood drawer box** with white melamine, predrilled metal shelf-pin holes, **DTC concealed undermount full-extension soft-close drawer glides**, and **six-way adjustable soft-close hinges**. It is frameless/full-overlay, uses a white-melamine exterior and interior, and carries TSCA VI, KCMA, CARB2, and NGBS Green certifications. The source calls out a universal-box/front-set two-package model.

The book’s order convention is **door-style code + cabinet product number**, such as `HW-W0930` for a High Glossy White 9 W × 30 H wall cabinet. Product dimensions and identity must therefore remain canonical at the master-code level while construction and finish are held on the relevant frameless door-style overlay.

The standard frameless wall cabinet box depth is **12 inches**. `W3012`, `W3312`, and `W3612` are 30/33/36 W × 12 H double-door cabinets; `W3015`, `W3315`, and `W3615` are 30/33/36 W × 15 H double-door cabinets; `W3018`, `W3318`, and `W3618` are 30/33/36 W × 18 H double-door cabinets. The 24-inch-high double doors (`W3024`, `W3324`, `W3624`) have one adjustable shelf. The 30-inch-high single-door group (`W0930`, `W1230`, `W1530`, `W1830`, `W2130`) has two shelves; the double-door group (`W2430`, `W2730`, `W3030`, `W3330`, `W3630`) also has two shelves.

Refrigerator-wall cabinets have **24-inch cabinet depth**: `W301224`/`W331224`/`W361224` are 30/33/36 W × 12 H, `W301824`/`W331824`/`W361824` are 30/33/36 W × 18 H, and `W302424`/`W332424`/`W362424` are 30/33/36 W × 24 H. These are exact outer cabinet dimensions; the printed door-front sizes do not replace the cabinet width/depth used by the Designer.

The 36-inch-high wall cabinet line uses 12-inch cabinet depth: single doors `W0936`, `W1236`, `W1536`, `W1836`, `W2136` have two shelves; double doors `W2436`, `W2736`, `W3036`, `W3336`, `W3636` have two shelves. `MB30` is a microwave base cabinet: **30 W × 34 1/2 H × 24 D**. `BWB18` is a base waste-basket cabinet: **18 W × 34 1/2 H × 24 D**. The full-height door base codes shown are `FHB09`, `FHB18`, `FHB24`, `FHB30`, and `FHB36`; each is 34 1/2 H × 24 D with its code-aligned width.

In each case the suffix documents 84/90/96-inch outer height. These are tall floor cabinets—not elevated wall cabinets—and retain their code-derived width, 24-inch depth, and source-defined height in plan/elevation geometry.

## Verified source facts from visual pages 29–33

Single oven pantries are 24 inches deep: `O308424`, `O309024`, `O309624` are 30 W × 84/90/96 H × 24 D, and `O338424`, `O339024`, `O339624` are 33 W × 84/90/96 H × 24 D. The source depicts their intended oven opening and lower drawer configuration; their external geometry remains the stated cabinet width × height × depth.

Vanity sink bases `VS24`, `VS27`, `VS30`, and `VS36` are **34 1/2 H × 21 D**. Vanity drawer bases `3VDB12`, `3VDB15`, and `3VDB18` are likewise 34 1/2 H × 21 D. Vanity combo cabinets `V30TDL`/`V30TDR` and `V36TDL`/`V36TDR` are 30/36 W × 34 1/2 H × 21 D, with left/right door-drawer orientation encoded in the suffix. This 21-inch vanity depth is a source-backed exception to the 24-inch kitchen-base depth and must be retained in the catalog and Designer footprint.

## Verified source facts from visual pages 34–38

Accessories use their documented own geometry rather than generic cabinet depths. `OCM8`, `TKC`, `SM8`, and `SHM` are 96-inch molding/toe-kick lengths. Fillers are `F342` and `F642` at 42-inch height, and `F396` and `F696` at 96-inch height. `DWR3` is a 3 W × 34 1/2 H × 24 D dishwasher panel. `REPV2496` and `REPV2796` are refrigerator end panels; `FPL3796` is a 96-inch finished panel.

The source differentiates structural panels: `BDEP` is a 34 1/2-inch base decorative end panel, `WDEP30`/`WDEP36`/`WDEP42` are wall decorative end panels, and `BSPR`/`BSPL`, `VSPR`/`VSPL`, `USPR96`/`USPL96`, `WSP30`, `WSP36R`/`WSP36L`, and `WSP42R`/`WSP42L` are side-specific finished base, vanity, pantry, or wall panels. `MOS3018` is a wall microwave open box with the 30 W × 18 H profile shown in the source. These components should retain panel/open-box identity and must not be represented as generic base cabinets.

## Verified source facts from visual pages 39–42

The final product diagram lists `Mini Base` (12 W × 24 1/2 H × 15 D), sample door `SD` (11 W × 15 H), and finished panel `FPL2496` (96 L × 24 W × 3/4 thick). The source warranty is a **12-month limited residential warranty** and expressly treats glass cabinet doors as tempered safety glass that requires professional installation and careful handling. This supports the existing glass-policy disclosure category, but it does not establish whether a distributor includes a glass insert in its sellable SKU; that remains controlled by the distributor-specific policy.
