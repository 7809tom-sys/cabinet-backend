# Cabinet Dealz — Affiliate Storefront Pricing Strategy

**Prepared for:** Cabinet Dealz ownership
**Prepared by:** Manus AI
**Purpose:** Recommend how to price the affiliate storefront program — what to charge an affiliate to launch and keep a fully branded, turnkey cabinet storefront — and how to structure it for a launch to a ~10,000-contact list.

---

## The Short Version

Charge an **annual licensing fee**, not a one-time setup fee, and make the real money on **order margin**. An annual license gives you recurring, predictable revenue that covers your ongoing cost of hosting, support, and catalog maintenance every year — while the per-order margin grows automatically as each affiliate sells more.

The recommendation below uses a **three-tier annual license** with a **founding-member launch offer** to seed your list, and a modest **per-order platform margin** that captures upside on your strongest affiliates without scaring off new ones at the door.

---

## What an Affiliate Is Actually Buying

It is important to anchor the price against the value delivered, not against the cost of a "website." An affiliate is not buying a web page; they are buying a complete, ready-to-run cabinet business:

- A **fully branded storefront** — their logo, their colors, their own web address.
- Access to the **entire Cabinet Dealz catalog**, kept current for them.
- The **2D kitchen design tool**, quoting, and order submission.
- **Bilingual (English + Spanish)** customer experience.
- **Hosting, updates, security, and support** — handled for them, every year.

Built independently, this is a multi-thousand-dollar project plus a developer on retainer. Framed that way, an annual license in the hundreds-to-low-thousands range is an easy decision for a serious affiliate, because a single completed kitchen order typically returns more than the license costs.

## Recommended Pricing Model

### Three annual license tiers

| Tier | Annual License | Best For | What It Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | **$499 / year** | New or part-time affiliates testing the program | Branded storefront, full catalog, 2D design tool, quote/order flow, English + Spanish, standard email support |
| **Pro** | **$1,500 / year** | Active dealers selling regularly | Everything in Starter, plus a **custom domain**, featured kitchen photography, priority onboarding and support, and lead routing |
| **Elite** | **$3,000 / year** | High-volume partners | Everything in Pro, plus **territory/exclusivity options**, marketing co-op support, and top placement |

The tiers do two jobs at once. The **$499 Starter** keeps the entry barrier low so you maximize sign-ups from a cold list, and the **Pro and Elite tiers raise your average revenue per affiliate** by giving your most committed partners a clear reason to pay more.

### The real lever: per-order platform margin

The license keeps the lights on; the **margin on every order** is your upside and scales with each affiliate's success. A small, transparent platform margin or per-order fee layered on top of the license means your best affiliates pay you far more than your casual ones — without ever feeling a high sticker price up front. This is the single most important structural choice in the model, because it ties your revenue to volume rather than to a fixed fee.

### Optional paid add-ons

À-la-carte upgrades lift average revenue per affiliate without raising the headline price: a custom domain, a premium photo package, local SEO setup, or a lead-generation boost. Each is a small additional charge that compounds across the network.

## Launch Strategy for the 10,000-Contact List

A warm list of 10,000 is the asset that makes this whole model work. Even a small conversion rate produces meaningful, recurring revenue before a single cabinet is sold. The goal at launch is **maximize sign-ups at the entry tier**, then grow each affiliate up the ladder and earn on their order volume over time.

### Founding-member offer (recommended)

Use a time-limited launch incentive to overcome first-mover hesitation and seed the network quickly:

- **Founding rate:** first year **$299** (renews at $499), **or**
- **Credit-back:** the license fee is **credited back after the affiliate's first $X in completed orders** — this removes the risk for them while protecting you if they never sell.

Either approach lowers the barrier for a cold audience while preserving the full-price anchor for everyone who joins after the launch window.

## What Happens at Non-Renewal

Because this is an annual license, renewal is also your natural retention filter. Affiliates who are selling will renew without hesitation; those who never got going will lapse — which is healthy. On non-renewal, the storefront should move to a **"renew to reactivate"** holding state rather than disappearing, so a lapsed affiliate can come back with one payment and an active customer never hits a dead link.

## Recommendation Summary

1. **Charge an annual license, not a one-time fee** — recurring revenue that matches your recurring cost.
2. **Use three tiers ($499 / $1,500 / $3,000)** so casual affiliates can enter cheaply while committed partners pay more for more.
3. **Layer a small per-order platform margin on top** — this is where the real, scalable money is.
4. **Launch with a founding-member offer** ($299 first year or fee-credited-back) to convert the 10,000-list fast.
5. **Add à-la-carte upgrades** (domain, photos, SEO, leads) to lift average revenue per affiliate.
6. **Lapse to a "renew to reactivate" state** so renewal doubles as your retention check.
