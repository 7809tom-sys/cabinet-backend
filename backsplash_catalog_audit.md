> **Preparation review:** The first assembled Carrara candidate preserved blank studio margins inside several chip crops. It is rejected. The revised deterministic extraction uses only complete marble-chip interiors from the verified product field before reconstructing the running bond, so no white product-photo background remains in the 3D texture.

> **Second preparation review:** The first revision still exposed partial-chip background at two staggered field edges. It is also rejected. The final extraction will source every repeated chip from the two fully visible top-row marble tiles only, retaining natural veining while eliminating all partial display chips and white surround.

> **Approved 3D source:** The final `carrara_subway_11_97x12_13_running_bond_f57029f6.webp` field was visually verified as a continuous Carrara marble running bond. It uses complete material-only chip interiors, has no staged room/photo-card background, repeats on the documented 11.97 × 12.13-inch module, and leaves fine 1/16-inch joints to the renderer. It is assigned to the exact `Carrara Subway` Backsplash record; the shopper-facing catalog image remains unchanged.

> **Cloudline source review:** Pelican’s primary `CloudlineMarble.png` is rejected as a 3D source because it is an installed/staged kitchen photograph with an espresso machine and counter scene. The alternate `CloudlineMarble.jpg` is suitable as controlled source material because it shows only the product sheet. Its white surround and incomplete edge chips must be excluded. The prepared 3D field may use only complete glass or marble chip interiors from that official source and must reconstruct the documented continuous staggered mosaic without carrying through white display margins.

## Audit rule

Every row must be verified from an official Pelican, manufacturer, or authorized distributor source before its pattern metadata or material-only 3D asset is assigned. A staged room, retail card, label, white border, or product-sheet frame is never an approved 3D texture source.
