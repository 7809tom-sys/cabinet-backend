# PL-8369 Source Evidence

## Official Manufacturer Sources

The Pelican International product page identifies **PL-8369** as a **Fountain Series Kitchen Pot Filler**, a wall-mounted fixture for filling cookware at the cooktop. It describes a dual-jointed swing arm that folds against the wall when not in use and lists Brushed Gold, Brushed Nickel, Matte Black, and Chrome variants. The page currently shows a starting consumer price of $399.99, but that public retail display is not used as an authorized CabinetDealz sell-price source.[1]

The official technical sheet documents a wall-mount installation, premium metal/lead-free brass valve body, ceramic-disc valves, cold-water connection, 2.2 gallons per minute maximum flow rate at 60 psi, and the following nominal geometry: 24-inch spout length, 26 3/16-inch overall span, 13-inch articulated-arm datum, 3 5/32-inch spout height, and 1 7/8-inch outlet datum.[2] The official installation sheet confirms an in-wall pipe connection, wall base/cover installation, articulated connection assembly, and required flushing/leak-check sequence.[3]

The imported `pelican_products` rows for PL-8369 (`id` 790–793) retain their positive authorized source list-price basis of **$98.99**. On 2026-09-25, their names, descriptions, finish identities, and catalog selection image were corrected from the official product/specification sources. The image is an official dimension drawing used only as catalog-selection art; it is never used as a 3D fixture model or material.

The duplicate source row `id` 789 used the base code `PL-8369` but was incorrectly named as an 18-inch bar sink. It was made inactive and hidden from the catalog and Designer rather than retitled or repriced. The customer-facing public retail display of $399.99 was not copied into any commercial pricing field.

Rows 790–793 remain excluded from the Designer. The product profile now preserves its measured wall fixture geometry and exact finish variants, but Pelican does not publish one universal installation height above the finished cooktop/counter. A future Designer workflow must require a project-specific documented mounting datum, attach the product only to the wall behind a valid range/cooktop, persist the range association and finish, and render the 24-inch articulated reach without treating catalog art as geometry.
