# Pelican Designer Readiness Audit — 2026-09-22

## Authoritative persisted product fields

`pelican_products` does not contain role, sell price, separate geometric dimensions, or a `designerEnabled` field. The persisted fields that govern the current Designer catalog are `category`, `subCategory`, `size`, `listPrice`, `yourCost`, `imageUrl`, `materialImageUrl`, `showInDesigner`, `showInCatalog`, `isActive`, `coverageSqFt`, and `unsupportedOverhangLimitIn`.

The public sell price is calculated server-side from the approved Pelican source cost (`yourCost`, falling back to `listPrice`) and the configured category markup. It is never a stored or manually invented product price.

## Read-only database findings

The 2026-09-22 read-only audit found that active, Designer-enabled countertop, flooring, tile, sink, faucet, and range-top records are generally source-priced. Range-top records have source prices but no `imageUrl`; the existing recommendation route correctly excludes them from the Designer rather than displaying an unsupported or invented asset.

## Readiness policy

- **Flooring, backsplash, and countertop:** must be active, Designer-enabled, image-backed, and have a positive calculated sell price. A catalog image is a selection-card image only. Surface material mapping continues to use only separately approved material representation.
- **Sinks and faucets:** meet the preceding availability and price conditions and must also resolve to a source-measured `verifiedPelicanSinkProfile` or `verifiedPelicanFaucetProfile`. Catalog thumbnails, room photos, and retail photography are never used as three-dimensional geometry or material textures.
- **Range tops:** remain absent from Designer recommendations until an authorized image and exact 3D placement/profile assets are available. Their existing source price is not exposed as a Designer selection without those assets.

This file is a temporary working audit note. The durable implementation and regression coverage live in `server/routers/surfaces.ts` and `server/routers/surfaces.recommended.test.ts`.
