# User-Supplied 3D Reference Notes

## Source

User-provided Cabinets.com layout screenshots in this session, including the layout-picking stage and a fully furnished kitchen 3D view. No public URL was supplied for these screenshots; they are visual references, not product specifications.

## Required visual qualities

The reference scene makes cabinet construction readable through face frames, recessed panels, consistent pulls, visible side skins, toe-kick recesses, countertop edges, dimensional islands, and credible door/window openings. Appliance realism depends on clear product-family construction: control location, cooktop, oven glass, handles, seams, feet, range hood form, and appliance proportions must follow the selected catalog variant rather than a generic appliance shape.

## Presentation requirements

The scene should remain easy to inspect through a clear three-quarter camera, translucent contextual room walls, visible floor and architectural edges, soft contact shadows, and warm/cool layered lighting. Cabinet Dealz must preserve its real catalog selection, 2D placement, price context, and collision rules while improving these visual details.
