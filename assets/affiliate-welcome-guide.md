**Congratulations on joining the Cabinet Dealz Affiliate Program.** This guide walks you through everything you need to know to get your branded storefront live, attract customers, and start earning commissions. Keep it handy — you will refer back to it often in your first few weeks.

## 3. Setting Up Your Brand (Do This First)

Before you share your storefront link with anyone, spend five minutes personalizing it. Go to the **Customize** tab in your dashboard and complete the following:

**Logo.** Upload a PNG or JPG of your company logo. It will appear in the header of your storefront and on all customer-facing pages. Recommended size: 400 × 120 pixels or wider.

**Tagline.** Write one sentence that describes your business. Example: *"Sarasota's trusted source for kitchen cabinets and design."* This appears below your store name on the storefront hero.

**Template.** Choose from Classic (traditional, navy and gold), Showcase (bold, image-forward), Minimal (clean, modern), or Craftsman (warm, wood-toned). You can switch templates at any time without losing your content.

**Hero Photo.** Upload a photo of a kitchen you have worked on, a showroom, or any high-quality kitchen scene. This is the large banner image at the top of your storefront. If you do not upload one, a professional stock kitchen photo is used automatically.

## 4. Sharing Your Storefront — Marketing That Works

Your storefront link is your most powerful sales tool. Here are the most effective ways to use it.

### Business Cards and Print Materials

Print your storefront URL on every business card, flyer, and estimate sheet. Use the short, clean format: `cabinetdealz.com/store/your-slug`. Customers can type it directly into any browser — no app download required.

### Social Media

Post your storefront link in your Instagram bio, Facebook page About section, and any other social profiles. When you post a kitchen photo, include your link in the caption. A simple caption like *"Tap the link in my bio to browse the full catalog and design your kitchen online"* drives consistent traffic.

### Google Business Profile

If you have a Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business), add your storefront URL as your website. Customers searching for cabinet dealers in your area will see your link directly in Google Search and Maps results.

### Email Signature

Add your storefront URL to your email signature. Every estimate, follow-up, and invoice you send becomes a passive marketing touchpoint.

### Text Messages

When a customer asks about cabinets, send them your link directly: *"Here's my online catalog — you can browse styles and even design your kitchen layout right there: cabinetdealz.com/store/your-slug"*. Most customers will click a link in a text within minutes.

### Referral Link vs. Storefront Link

You have two links:

- **Storefront link** (`/store/your-slug`) — sends customers to your branded homepage. Best for first impressions and general sharing.
- **Referral link** (`/order/new?ref=YOUR-CODE`) — sends customers directly to the order/quote form with your code pre-filled. Best for warm leads who are ready to buy.

Both links credit all orders to your account.

## 5. Using the CRM to Close More Sales

Your CRM (Customer Relationship Management) board is built into your dashboard under the **CRM** tab. It works like a simple task board — you create cards for each lead and move them through stages as the conversation progresses.

**Recommended stages to use:**

| Stage | When to Use It |
|---|---|
| New Lead | Someone expressed interest — a text, a call, a social media DM |
| Contacted | You have reached out and are waiting for a response |
| Measuring / Quoting | You are actively working on their kitchen dimensions or quote |
| Proposal Sent | You sent them a quote or storefront link |
| Closed — Won | They placed an order through your storefront |
| Closed — Lost | They went elsewhere or the project was cancelled |

**Best practices for your CRM:**

Every new inquiry should become a CRM card the same day. Include the customer's name, phone number, and a note about what they are looking for (e.g., "3-bedroom kitchen remodel, white shaker style, budget around $8,000"). The more detail you add early, the easier follow-up becomes.

Set a follow-up reminder for every card that is not yet closed. Most kitchen sales take two to four weeks from first contact to order. Consistent follow-up — a text or call every three to five days — is the single biggest driver of closed deals.

When a customer places an order through your storefront, mark their CRM card as Closed — Won and note the order total. Over time, your CRM becomes a record of your sales history and a source of repeat business.

## 6. Understanding Commissions

You earn a commission on every confirmed order that comes through your storefront or referral link. Your commission rate is set by Cabinet Dealz and is shown on your dashboard.

**How it works:**

1. A customer visits your storefront and places an order.
2. The order is fulfilled and confirmed by the supply network.
3. Your commission is calculated on the confirmed order total and added to your Payouts balance.
4. Payouts are processed on a regular schedule — check the Payouts tab for your current balance and history.

There is no cap on how much you can earn. A single kitchen order typically ranges from $3,000 to $15,000 in product value. At a 5% commission rate, one kitchen order earns you $150 to $750.

Access the design tool at **cabinetdealz.com/design** or through the Design link on your storefront.
