## Conclusion

The trim photos that ARE present generally have correct file names matching their SKU codes. However, there are **9 items with potentially incorrect or shared photos** (highlighted above), and **22 UCD catalog trim items that are completely missing** from the database. The most impactful issues are the shared photos for WSV-24/30/36 (all showing the 42" version) and RS-15 (showing the 18" version).
