# Altaeuro / U.S. Cabinet Depot filler availability decision — 18 Oct 2026

## Decision

Do **not** add a Luxe Select `LSP` filler to the priced Designer catalog at this time. The Designer rule requires every selectable item to carry an exact source-backed price; the available official material establishes neither an LSP filler SKU nor an LSP price.

## What the official source proves

The U.S. Cabinet Depot *Altaeuro Pricing & Availability, V4.2026* (pp. 25–26) lists 3-inch and 6-inch overlay-filler assemblies for the quick-ship finishes `TWP`, `TGW`, `TDW`, and `PGW` only. It identifies each stock assembly as:

- `OF{width}{height}` cabinet-level filler;
- matching door-material `FOL{width}{height}` overlay; and
- grey-linen `WG-F{width}{height}` backer.

It prints the following finished sizes:

- 3-inch family: 2-7/8 in wide × 22-1/2, 30-1/8, 35-1/8, 40-1/8, 83-3/4, 88-3/4, or 93-3/4 in high × 3/4 in deep.
- 6-inch family: 5-7/8 in wide × 30-1/8, 35-1/8, 40-1/8, 83-3/4, 88-3/4, or 93-3/4 in high × 3/4 in deep.

The Studio Series specification sheet identifies `LSP` as Luxe Slab Picasso 1, a made-to-order Studio finish that fits the Altaeuro box. It does **not** identify a matching filler assembly or price. The P&A source contains no `LSP`/Studio filler row.

## Required evidence before LSP can become selectable

An official Studio dealer price export/order form that explicitly maps LSP to the exact OF/FOL/WG-F assembly and its price. Do not infer LSP from the TWP/TGW/TDW/PGW rows and do not use a generic price.
