## Verified source facts from visual pages 3–7

The Builder Grade line uses full-overlay five-piece solid-wood doors and drawer fronts with MDF center panels, a 3/4-inch solid-wood face frame, a 1/2-inch cabinet-grade furniture-board box, plywood rebate-joint drawer boxes, builder-grade undermount drawer glides, and six-way adjustable non-soft-close hinges. Exterior finish is matching color; interior finish is natural melamine. Shelves are 5/8-inch furniture board with natural-melamine finish and edge banding on all sides. The source also specifies door bumpers, metal shelf pins/clips, an L bracket for base cabinets, one package per cabinet, and TSCA VI/KCMA/CARB2/NGBS Green certification.

This is construction/style metadata. It must be stored only on the Builder Grade supplier style overlays; it does not change canonical master SKU dimensions or invent prices.

## Verified source facts from visual pages 9–13

Builder Grade standard wall boxes are 12 inches deep. W3012/W3312/W3612, W3015/W3615, and W3018/W3318/W3618 have no adjustable shelf. W3024/W3324/W3624 have one adjustable shelf. Standard 30- and 36-inch-high walls have two adjustable shelves; 42-inch-high walls have three. The source includes W3930, a 39-W × 30-H × 12-D double-door wall cabinet, so any future supplier source row must resolve to a true 39-inch master SKU rather than a 36-inch substitute.

Builder Grade refrigerator uppers are W361224/W361824/W362424: each is 36 W × 12/18/24 H × 24 D and has no adjustable shelf. This book does not support a generic 30- or 33-inch refrigerator-upper substitute for the Builder Grade source line.
